Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Mithkar is a daily adhkar and habits app designed to work offline. It requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your daily progress, streaks, habits, and settings are stored locally on your device. Widgets access this information through an on-device shared container. We do not upload it to a server and cannot access it.

Location

When you enable prayer times, your coordinates—latitude and longitude—are sent over an encrypted connection to AlAdhan.com to retrieve accurate times. No personal identifier accompanies the request, and the app keeps no history of your locations. You can turn off location access at any time in the app or system settings; Mithkar will then calculate prayer times on your device.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They never pass through a server.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect personal information or usage data: no names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or usage logs.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if this policy changes materially. For questions, get in touch.
