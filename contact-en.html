What we can help with

  • Feedback and suggestions for the app
  • Bug reports and technical problems
  • Notes on the adhkar texts or their sources
  • Privacy and data questions

Reporting a problem

To get help faster, please include:

  • Your device model — for example, iPhone 15 Pro
  • Your iOS version
  • The app version, found in Mithkar's settings
  • What happened, and what you expected instead

A note about your data

Mithkar collects no personal data, and everything you record — progress, streaks, habits — stays on your device alone. That means we cannot look up or restore your data for you. See the privacy policy for details.
